Phase-by-phase reduction plan
Each phase is a target range — your actual pace may be faster or slower. That is completely fine. The goal is arriving, not speed.
Phase 1 · Months 1–3
2,700 → 2,400 mg
Drop one 100mg reduction every 2–3 weeks. Start by removing 100mg from the smallest dose of the day. Build confidence that drops are manageable.
Phase 2 · Months 3–6
2,400 → 1,800 mg
Continue 100mg drops every 2–3 weeks. Supplements should be well established by now. CBD and Lion's Mane filling the gap.
Phase 3 · Months 6–9
1,800 → 1,200 mg
The middle phase. Nerve repair supplements doing significant work now. Exercises helping maintain mobility and nerve signalling.
Phase 4 · Months 9–12
1,200 → 900 mg
Getting into the lower range. Sleep and anxiety support from Reishi and magnesium especially important here. Vagus nerve work daily.
Phase 5 · Months 12–15
900 → 600 mg
Approaching the 80% reduction goal. Drops may feel harder here — go even slower if needed. 50mg drops are valid. You are almost there.
Phase 6 · Month 15–18
600 → 540 mg
Goal reached. 80% reduction. Celebrate this. Decide with your doctor whether to continue reducing or hold here as your maintenance dose.
What to expect after each drop
Days 1–3
Possibly nothing. Or mild anxiety, restlessness, slightly worse sleep. This is normal and temporary.
Days 4–7
Peak adjustment period. Use valerian for sleep, L-Theanine for anxiety, CBD for pain. Stay consistent with all supplements.
Week 2+
Body settles at the new dose. Most people feel stable again. This is the green light to plan the next small drop.

A note on these stories
Success stories are underrepresented online because people who get better tend to move on with their lives rather than posting about it. The people still posting are often still struggling. Important notes
Magnesium timing rule
Always take magnesium at least 2–3 hours before or after any gabapentin dose. Taking them together reduces how much gabapentin your body absorbs.
Blood pressure watch
CBD can temporarily affect BP — track it around CBD dose changes. Gabapentin withdrawal can also spike BP. Always tell your doctor what supplements you're taking.
Start slow
Add one new supplement every 3–5 days so you can identify what helps and what doesn't agree with you.
Give it time
Lion's Mane and Reishi need 4–8 weeks to reach full effect. ALA and CBD work faster. Track in your daily log so you can see what's shifting.
Daily movement protocol
Exercises for nerve health & recovery
Designed for nerve repair, foot and ankle mobility, hand dexterity, and nervous system regulation. Do what you can — even 5 minutes counts.
🦶
Ankle alphabet
Restores full ankle mobility and stimulates nerve pathways after foot surgery.
  • Sit with your foot lifted off the floor
  • Use your big toe as a "pen," trace A–Z in the air
  • Move only your ankle — keep your leg still
  • Do both feet. Start with 1–2 rounds
  • Best done morning and evening
🦵
Nerve flossing
Mobilises the sciatic nerve through its full range, reducing pain and improving nerve signal conduction.
  • Sit on edge of chair, back straight
  • Slowly extend one leg — point toes toward ceiling
  • Hold 2 seconds, then lower and point toes down
  • Repeat 10–15 times per leg, slow and controlled
  • Stop if sharp pain — mild stretch is normal
✋
Right hand dexterity
Stimulates nerve pathways in the hand, improving coordination and reducing neuropathic symptoms.
  • Thumb to each fingertip in sequence — repeat 10x
  • Spread fingers wide, then gentle fist — 10x
  • Pick up small objects (coins, buttons) one by one
  • Write or trace letters on paper
  • Squeeze a soft stress ball 30 seconds, release
🧠
Vagus nerve meditation
Activating the vagus nerve reduces pain signals, lowers anxiety, and calms withdrawal symptoms. One of the most powerful free tools available.
  • Sit comfortably or lie down
  • Use headphones if you have them
  • Let the guided audio do the work
  • Even 10 minutes makes a difference
